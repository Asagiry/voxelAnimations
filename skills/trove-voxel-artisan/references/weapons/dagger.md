# Archetype Reference: Daggers & Dual Blades

Use this reference whenever authoring any Dagger, Kunai, Kris, Assassin Dirk, or Dual Blades in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Blade**:
  - Length: 35–50 voxels (~0.45m – 0.65m).
  - Breadth: 4–7 voxels broad at the base, tapering sharply to an acute needle-point tip.
  - Profile: Double-edged diamond cross-section, sinister wavy flame profile (Kris), or recurve sickle profile with blood gutters (*fullers*).
  - Thickness: 2–3 voxels along the center spine ridge, stepped down to 1-voxel cutting edges.
* **Hilt & Reverse Grip**:
  - Length: 16–22 voxels, optimized for either orthodox or reverse assassin grip.
  - Guard: Compact crossguard, winged quillons, or circular knuckle-ring (for spinning finger tricks).
  - Pommel: Spiked glass-breaker skull pommel or weighted counterbalance ring.
* **Secondary Accents**:
  - Poison drip trails (toxic emerald glow), shadow mist ribbons, or spectral phantom blade echoes.

---

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: The Flurry & Fatal Puncture

Daggers embody **furious close-quarters agility, deceptive reverse grips, and lethal precision**:

```
[Phase 1: Shadow Stalker Ready] (Frames 1–12)
    └── Low, predatory reverse-grip posture. Blades angle backward along the forearms.
    └── Subtle dark mist motes billow softly from the poison fullers.

[Phase 2: Lightning Feint & Wind-up] (Frames 13–20)
    └── Sudden twitch step: blades pivot in hand to orthodox forward grip (quick 180° wrist flip).
    └── Kinetic coil: arms pull back tight against the chest.

[Phase 3: Supersonic Dual Cross-Slash & Puncture] (Frames 21–28)
    └── Explosive X-shaped cross-slash followed by an instantaneous piercing thrust!
    └── Dual luminous slash ribbons trace the razor-thin geometric cut paths.

[Phase 4: Zanshin Impact Snap] (Frames 29–38)
    └── Deadlock freeze at full thrust extension. Blades vibrate with high-frequency kinetic tension.
    └── Slashing trails detonate into tiny micro-voxel shadow sparks.

[Phase 5: Blade Spin Reset] (Frames 39–60)
    └── Acrobatic twirl spinning the daggers back into the reverse stealth grip along the forearms. Seamless loop.
```

---

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Configuration: single primary dagger vs. paired dual-wield daggers with asymmetrical silhouettes.
- Aesthetics: sleek futuristic cyber-kunai, corrupted abyssal bone daggers, or gilded venomous kris.
- Grip orientation: orthodox forward thrust vs. reverse backhand assassin stance.
- Just adhere strictly to the choreography: **Predatory Stance -> Grip Pivot -> Lightning Cross-Slash -> Frozen Puncture -> Acrobatic Spin Reset**.
