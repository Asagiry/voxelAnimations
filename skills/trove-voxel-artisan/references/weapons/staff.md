# Archetype Reference: Magic Staves, Scepters & Catalysts

Use this reference whenever authoring any Magic Staff, Archmage Scepter, Necromancer Wand, or Elemental Catalyst in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Shaft (Runic Rod)**:
  - Height: 85–115 voxels tall (~1.0m – 1.35m).
  - Aged twisted heartwood, faceted obsidian stone, or filigree gold with spiral channel inlays.
* **Headpiece / Crown (The Focal Nexus)**:
  - Height: 25–45 voxels tall; distinct silhouette: dragon talons, crescent moon, astrological armillary rings, or a crystalline spire.
  - **Levitating Magical Core**: The center of the crown houses a freely floating power source (a faceted mana gem, void eye, or elemental flame orb).
* **Base Ferrule**:
  - Pointed metallic ferrule or grounding rune stone at the base.

---

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: Spell Channeling & Detonation

A magic staff channels **arcane resonance, fluid acceleration, and impulse backblast**:

```
[Phase 1: Serene Levitation] (Frames 1–16)
    └── Staff hovers vertically. The crown crystal rotates slowly, emitting soft motes of ethereal dust.

[Phase 2: Arcane Channeling] (Frames 17–30)
    └── Staff tilts forward into casting orientation. The floating core and crown rings accelerate into a high-speed spin, drawing in surrounding magical embers.

[Phase 3: Spell Detonation / Thrust] (Frames 31–38)
    └── Sudden forward thrust! A geometric arcane seal or dimensional portal erupts ahead of the crown, firing an explosive energy beam or shockwave.

[Phase 4: Resonance Backblast Absorption] (Frames 39–48)
    └── The staff absorbs the backward recoil of the blast; the crown rings tilt and resonate with residual heat/light.

[Phase 5: Core Settling & Recovery] (Frames 49–60)
    └── The core decelerates back into calm orbit; the staff rises back to its serene vertical posture. Seamless loop.
```

---

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Elemental alignment: solar radiance, abyss void, cryo-ice, demonic blood, or verdant nature.
- Crown geometry: hovering crystal spires, revolving planetary rings, or ornate beast talons.
- Just adhere strictly to the channeling kinetics: **Serene Levitation -> Core Acceleration -> Forward Thrust Burst -> Backblast Absorption -> Calm Reset**.
