# Archetype Reference: Katana & Iaido Curved Blades

Use this reference whenever authoring any Katana, Odachi, Wakizashi, Tachi, or Iaido curved sword in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Curved Blade (*Sori* Curvature)**:
  - Length: 85–105 voxels long (~1.0m – 1.25m).
  - Breadth: 4–6 voxels wide at the *habaki* collar, tapering gracefully to 3–4 voxels at the tip (*kissaki*).
  - Thickness: 2–3 voxels thick at the spine (*mune*), stepping down to a 1-voxel razor edge (*ha*).
  - Subtle back curvature (*sori* offset: 4–7 voxels along the spine). Never render a katana as a straight iron stick.
  - Differential temper line (*hamon*): An undulating, crystalline wave pattern along the sharp cutting edge using a lighter/luminous voxel tier.
* **Tsuba Guard**:
  - 8×8 to 12×12 voxels circular, octagonal, or square disc, 2 voxels thick.
  - Features openwork pierced cutouts (*sukashi*) with a contrasting gilded brass or dark blackened iron rim.
* **Tsuka Handle**:
  - Length: 24–32 voxels long, 3×3 voxels thick.
  - White granulated rayskin underlay (*samegawa*) visible through a diamond-pattern criss-cross silk cord wrap (*ito*).
  - Gilded dragon, cherry blossom, or lightning crest ornaments (*menuki*) tucked under the wrap.

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: The Iaido Crescent Slash

The katana embodies **hyper-speed, razor discipline, and clean geometry**:

```
[Phase 1: Ready Focus Stance] (Frames 1–15)
    └── Low, grounded ready posture. Blade angled downward or resting in an ethereal scabbard.
    └── Absolute stillness and subtle breathing; energy coils silently in the blade.

[Phase 2: Supersonic Crescent Slash] (Frames 16–24)
    └── Blindingly fast drawing slash from lower hip across in a rising diagonal or horizontal crescent arc.
    └── A continuous, solid micro-voxel energy ribbon ignites along the entire cutting edge, tracing the crescent geometry of the slice.

[Phase 3: Zanshin Combat Freeze] (Frames 25–36)
    └── The blade locks instantaneously into a frozen, resolute execution posture at the apex of the cut.
    └── The luminous crescent slash ribbon lingers in the air before softly dissolving.

[Phase 4: Chiburui Wrist Snap] (Frames 37–48)
    └── A crisp, ritualistic snap of the wrist to flick residual blood and embers from the steel.

[Phase 5: Noto Sheathing] (Frames 49–60)
    └── A slow, deliberate, meditative glide returning the blade to its scabbard/ready angle. Seamless loop.
```

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Scale: compact, razor-fast Wakizashi vs. colossal, sweeping two-handed Odachi.
- Elemental theme: icy cyan rime, cherry blossom petals, dark blood embers, or celestial thunderbolts.
- Material finishes: folded tamahagane Damascus steel, gilded imperial brass, obsidian spine, crystalline hamon.
- Just adhere strictly to the choreography: **Stillness -> Supersonic Crescent Slash -> Zanshin Freeze -> Chiburui Snap -> Deliberate Noto Return**.
