# Archetype Reference: Bows & Ranged Weapons

Use this reference whenever authoring any Bow, Recurve Bow, Celestial Greatbow, or Crystal Crossbow in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Limbs (Recurve Arcs & Wings)**:
  - Height: 90–120 voxels tall (~1.1m – 1.45m).
  - Sweeping stepped curves: dragon horns, layered eagle plumage, or crystalline wing tiers (3–5 voxels thick at the riser, tapering gracefully to 2 voxels at the tips).
* **Riser (Grip)**:
  - Ergonomic central grip with an integrated arrow shelf and decorative rune inlays.
* **Bowstring**:
  - A taut 1-voxel luminous energy cord or braided sinew anchored to top and bottom limb tips.
* **Arrow / Ammunition**:
  - Faceted diamond crystal arrowhead, solid wooden or astral shaft (60–75 voxels), and glowing triangular fletching.

---

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: The Draw, Release & Snap

A bow comes alive through **accumulated elastic strain and explosive release**:

```
[Phase 1: Idle Float & Energy Hum] (Frames 1–12)
    └── Bow hovers vertically; glowing bowstring softly hums. An ethereal arrow gently levitates alongside the riser.

[Phase 2: Full Draw Tension] (Frames 13–34)
    └── The limbs flex backward into deep tension; the bowstring pulls into an acute triangle.
    └── Arrow locks onto the rest. The entire assembly trembles subtly under maximum mechanical and magical potential energy.

[Phase 3: Release & Supersonic Flight] (Frames 35–37)
    └── Instantaneous release! The arrow shoots forward with blinding velocity, trailing braided starlight ribbons.

[Phase 4: Damped Limb Vibration] (Frames 38–45)
    └── The limbs and bowstring snap forward and oscillate in a rapidly diminishing sinusoidal vibration, absorbing the backblast.

[Phase 5: Arrow Rematerialization] (Frames 46–60)
    └── A new crystal arrow materializes from ambient starlight/energy particles onto the bow, resetting the loop. Seamless loop.
```

---

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Limb silhouettes: phoenix wings, glacial ice spikes, elven golden branches, or mechanical gear-pulley cams.
- Elemental energy: celestial starlight, void plasma, frost needles, or solar fire.
- Just adhere strictly to the elastic kinetics: **Idle Hover -> Full Draw Tremor -> Instantaneous Release -> Damped Vibration -> Rematerialization**.
