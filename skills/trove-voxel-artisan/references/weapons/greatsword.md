# Archetype Reference: Colossal Greatswords & Ultra-Heavy Blades

Use this reference whenever authoring any Greatsword, Ultra-Greatsword, Dragon Slayer (Guts), Zweihander, or Executioner Blade in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Monolithic Slab Proportions**:
  - *"It was too big to be called a sword... It was more like a raw heap of iron."*
  - Length: 95–130 voxels (~1.2m – 1.6m).
  - Width: 14–24 voxels broad! A towering, intimidating mass.
  - Thickness: 4–8 voxels thick along the central spine ridge, stepped down to 2–1 voxels on heavy beveled bevels with battle notches, chips, and forged hammer marks.
* **Hilt & Guard**:
  - Long two-handed grip: 28–36 voxels long, bound in weathered bandages, dark leather wraps, or chain links.
  - Heavy riveted iron crossguard or beast-jaw collar (4–6 voxels thick, secured by forged bolts).
  - Pommel: Massive faceted iron counterweight.

---

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: The Ground-Shattering Berserk Slam

Heavy greatswords strike with **raw mass, gravity, and apocalyptic momentum**:

```
[Phase 1: Heroic Shoulder Rest] (Frames 1–15)
    └── The massive slab rests comfortably across the shoulder at a 35° angle, gently bobbing with the wielder's breathing.

[Phase 2: Two-Handed Heavy Hoist] (Frames 16–28)
    └── Slow, strained, muscular lift raising the colossal iron slab high above the head.
    └── Heavy kinetic anticipation; a brief, tense pause at the apex as gravity prepares to take over.

[Phase 3: Earth-Shattering Cleave] (Frames 29–34)
    └── Violent, downward gravitational collapse crashing straight into the granite ground.

[Phase 4: Shockwave & Shrapnel Eruption] (Frames 35–42)
    └── At the point of impact, stone fractures, sparks, and blood/lava bursts erupt in all directions.
    └── The steel slab vibrates from the immense concussive impact against the rock.

[Phase 5: Extraction & Reset] (Frames 43–60)
    └── Heavy levering of the blade out of the fractured ground and smoothly resetting it back onto the shoulder. Seamless loop.
```

---

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Material treatment: raw pitted iron, glowing volcanic magma veins, ancient runic granite, or dragon bone.
- Guard & pommel styling: brutalist spiked iron collar vs. gothic filigree with chained talismans.
- Impact VFX: exploding stone shrapnel, localized seismic craters, erupting geysers of fire.
- Just adhere strictly to the weight kinetics: **Shoulder Rest -> Heavy Muscular Hoist -> Gravitational Slam -> Shrapnel Burst -> Heavy Reset**.
