# Archetype Reference: Warhammers, Greathammers & Maces

Use this reference whenever authoring any Warhammer, Greathammer, Earthshaker Maul, Flanged Mace, or Meteor Hammer in Trove/Cube World micro-voxel style.

---

## 1. Anatomy & Voxel Proportions

* **Shaft (Haft)**:
  - Length: 75–105 voxels long (~0.9m – 1.3m).
  - Cross-section: 3×3 to 4×4 voxels thick. Reinforced with iron splints (*langets*), bronze rivets, and coarse leather wraps.
* **Hammer Head (Top-Heavy Kinetic Mass)**:
  - Massive volumetric block: 16–28 voxels long, 12–20 voxels wide, 12–20 voxels high.
  - Striking face: Heavy flat textured waffle grid, brutalist pyramid spike, or multifaceted skull motif.
  - Reverse side: Piercing war-pick (*beak*) or counterweight block.
  - Center nexus: Molten forge engine, embedded runic geode, or glowing seismic reactor core.
* **Base Butt-Spike / Counterweight**:
  - Heavy flared pommel or secondary striking spike to balance the immense forward center of mass.

---

## 2. Combat Physics & Animation Choreography: The Seismic Concussion Slam

A warhammer strikes with **unforgiving top-heavy inertia, crushing weight, and radial ground rupture**:

```
[Phase 1: Ground Rest / Low Guard] (Frames 1–14)
    └── The massive head rests firmly on the earth or is held low in a two-handed diagonal guard.
    └── Molten veins or seismic runes inside the hammer head pulse with a deep, low-frequency cadence.

[Phase 2: Strained Overhead Hoist] (Frames 15–28)
    └── Strenuous two-handed heave lifting the colossal mass overhead.
    └── Noticeable center-of-gravity shift; the weapon leads the body's momentum into a wide upward arc.

[Phase 3: Cataclysmic Downward Slam] (Frames 29–34)
    └── Pure gravitational collapse! The hammer crashes down at maximum velocity onto the ground plane.
    └── The striking face impacts flat against the earth.

[Phase 4: Shockwave Detonation & Structural Shudder] (Frames 35–44)
    └── Radial shockwave erupts: expanding micro-voxel dust rings, flying fractured stone debris, and erupting magma fissures.
    └── The haft shudders from the concussive force; the hammer head stays planted in the crushed crater.

[Phase 5: Heavy Earth Extraction & Reset] (Frames 45–60)
    └── Muscular heave prying the heavy head out of the shattered impact crater and smoothly swinging back into low guard. Seamless loop.
```

---

## 3. Creative Freedom Guidelines

You have complete artistic license to design:
- Theme: volcanic magma forge hammer, thunderous titan mallet, brutalist barbed iron maul, or celestial gravity warhammer.
- Secondary VFX: ring of jagged stone spikes, crackling lightning arcs between the haft and head, or geysers of molten sparks.
- Head shape: symmetrical dual-faced forge anvil vs. asymmetrical war-pick hammer.
- Just adhere strictly to the inertia kinematics: **Low Guard -> Strained Hoist -> Gravitational Slam -> Concussion Shockwave -> Crater Extraction Reset**.
